About Dara

Company Overview

Dara Everbright Ltd is a private company limited by shares, co-founded by Dela Aniwa and Ruth Adjei in September 2025. The company is built to be a multi-sector holdings firm, with flexibility to branch into different industries while keeping a core focus on media consultancy and production as the flagship service.

What Sets Dara Everbright Apart

Rooted in Missionary Experience – using the discipline, communication, and media skills developed during missionary and MTC service.

Professional & Relatable Content – a balance of clarity, storytelling, and modern design.

Growth Vision – starting lean (with media consultancy) but structured for long-term expansion.

Our Central Goal

To become a trusted media consultancy and production house that empowers organizations, brands, and individuals with creative, impactful, and professional content.

We aim to help organizations communicate better through high-quality videography, editing, and digital media strategies, building a brand of excellence that reflects innovation, trust, and long-term value.
